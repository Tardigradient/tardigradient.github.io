FAQ

What will I do in this treasure hunt?
Use the map and the riddle that you receive to lead you along a path of clues that will help you find the hidden treasure chest! Feel free to work with a friend to crack the code, or go at it alone.

What's in the chest?
The exact contents of the chest are a secret, but they are worth more than a bit of effort! The chest includes approximately $50-$75 worth of gifts provided by local restaurants, as well as some other things.

Where might the treasure be hidden?
The treasure is hidden somewhere on the USU campus. You will not need to leave campus at any time in order to find it. Most of the places you need to go are outdoors, so dress warm!

Where is the money going?
This is a fundraiser for the Pirate Club to help us raise money for our activities. We usually do funny little games and competitions, watch movies, or make pirate stuff, with soda and snacks provided. We are also hoping to have enough money to build a raft at the end of the semester and float around on a lake! Here are some pictures of what we are talking about: Raft Pics.

When does the treasure hunt take place?
The treasure hunt will officially begin on March 1st, 2026. Registration is open and will remain open for the duration of the treasure hunt, which will of course end when somebody discovers the location of the chest.

Can I start hunting right now?
We will not put out the treasure chest or any of the clues until March 1, but perhaps you could ponder upon the initial riddle until then.

How hard is the treasure hunt?
It's difficult. We expect it to last a little into March before someone finds the treasure. We will release occasional hints via email. For an example of what you could be dealing with and the difficulty level we were sort of aiming for, check out the Utah Treasure Hunt.
