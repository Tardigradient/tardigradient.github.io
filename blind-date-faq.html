FAQ

What will we do in this treasure hunt?
Use the map and the riddle that you receive to lead you along a path of clues that will help you find the hidden treasure chest! Meanwhile, get to know someone new.

What's in the chest?
The exact contents of the chest are a secret, but they are worth more than a bit of effort! The chest includes approximately $50-$75 worth of gift cards and other things.

What if I can't make it to the March 1 meet-up?
Unfortunately, March 1 at 5:00 PM is the only time we will be facilitating meet-ups. If you can't make it, you need to inform Caleb Morgan as soon as possible. If you can't come but still want to participate in the treasure hunt, feel free to register normally (rather than through the blind date activity) by checking out this page. You can still sign up with a partner (or a date!) for $6 if you'd like, or you can just go at it alone for $4.

Where might the treasure be hidden?
The treasure is hidden somewhere on the USU campus. You will not need to leave campus at any time in order to find it. Most of the places you need to go are outdoors, so dress warm!

Where is the money going?
This is a fundraiser for the Pirate Club to help us raise money for our activities. We usually do funny little games and competitions, watch movies, or make piratey stuff, with soda and snacks provided. We are also hoping to save enough money to build a raft at the end of the semester and float around on a lake! Here are some pictures of what we are talking about: Raft Pics.

When does the treasure hunt take place?
The treasure hunt will officially begin on March 1st, 2026. General registration is open and will remain open for the duration of the treasure hunt, which will of course end when somebody discovers the location of the chest. Blind date registration is now closed.

Can we start hunting right now?
For the blind date activity, you will need to come to the March 1 meet-up to get your treasure map. None of the treasure hunt locations will be set up on campus until then. For general registration, you will receive the first clue in your email shortly after registering, and you can consider what location that clue might be referring to until March 1, when the rest of the clues will be released.

How hard is the treasure hunt?
It's difficult. We expect it to last at least a ways into March before someone finds the treasure. We will release occasional hints via email. For an example of what you could be dealing with and the difficulty level we were sort of aiming for, check out the Utah Treasure Hunt.

Who is in charge of this?
This activity was put together by the Pirate Club for a fundraiser, and we are collaborating with the Blind Date Club to help people get to know each other with a fun activity!

Heaven forbid, my date and I don't get along and we wanna drop out?
If things are going poorly, you can drop out at any time, or split up and each work on the treasure hunt individually. We will not be giving refunds, but if you do split up, remember that you paid the discounted price for the blind date as opposed to a single entry. You can carry on by yourself for cheaper than everyone else! Or get a friend to register to join you for $4.
