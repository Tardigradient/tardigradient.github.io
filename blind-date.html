How It Works

A Blind Date Club x Pirate Club Adventure

Thank you for your interest in the treasure hunt! We appreciate the support! Here is how this activity works:

  1. Fill out the registration form and get matched!
  2. If you are matched, you will receive an email. Come to the meet-up (March 1 at 5:00 PM) to meet your date and get the treasure map!
  3. Start hunting! Work on your own time, together with your new friend, and find the hidden chest.

We also invite you to join us at our 7:00 meetings every other Tuesday at LSB 231! Search us up on MyUSU or join our GroupMe for more info.

Rules

  • Please do not share the map or clues with others.
  • Don't move or tamper with any clues, and make sure you put everything back just how you found it so everyone can have a fair chance!
  • Take a photo at each location so we can confirm that you reached the chest legitimately. Skipping ahead will result in disqualification.
  • Do not try to open the chest. If you find it, contact us ASAP to arrange a time to unlock it.
  • Keep in mind that this is a competition. Only the first to find the chest will receive the prize!
