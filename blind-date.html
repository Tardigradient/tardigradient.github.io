USU Treasure Hunt

A Blind Date Club x Pirate Club Adventure

Thank you for your interest in the treasure hunt! We appreciate the support! This is not your average blind date. In fact, it's more like several dates. Here is how this activity works:

  1. This will be an ongoing event, starting on March 1 and ending when the treasure chest is found. You will have to work together with your date for as long as it takes to find the treasure! That could mean multiple weeks. Whatever you want to make it!
  2. If that sounds like fun, fill out the registration form in the Blind Date Club GroupMe and get matched!
  3. If you are matched, you will receive an email. Come to the meet-up (March 1 at 5:00 PM) to meet your date, hand in your $6 donation, and get the treasure map!
  4. Start hunting! Work on your own time, together with your new friend, and find the hidden chest.

Rules

By signing up for this activity, you agree to play by these rules:

  • You commit to show up to the meet-up and meet your date, then work with them to complete the treasure hunt. If you bail out or ghost without notice, you will be banned from the Blind Date Club.
  • Please do not share the map or clues with others. You are free to work with any of your friends, but we kindly ask that each participant submits the $4 entry amount.
  • Don't move or tamper with any clues, and make sure you put everything back just how you found it so everyone can have a fair chance!
  • Take a photo at each location so we can confirm that you reached the chest legitimately. Skipping ahead will result in disqualification.
  • Do not try to open the chest. If you find it, contact us ASAP to arrange a time to unlock it.
  • Keep in mind that this is a competition. Only the first to find the chest will receive the prize!
